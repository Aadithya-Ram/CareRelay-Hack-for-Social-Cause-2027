The problem

During a breakdown or outage, the nearest open clinic may lack compatible machines, staff, accessible treatment routes or a suitable slot. A transport suggestion is also not a provider commitment.

The approach

CareRelay combines staff-verified patient requirements, timestamped facility resources, suitable funded transport and explicit acceptance in one shared recovery workflow.

01

Match

Compatible treatment slots, staff and transport within supplied windows.

02

Commit

Facility acceptance reserves a slot; the provider confirms a suitable trip.

03

Track

Departure, arrival, treatment start and completion remain separate.

04

Repair

Preserve valid commitments; expose actionable unresolved needs.

Working implementation

React and TypeScript interface; server API on Vinext and Cloudflare Workers; D1/SQLite persistence with atomic revision checks. Four demo roles, registration, resource editing, facility acceptance, transport confirmation, disruption repair, rule-based message drafts, intervention tests and an independent practice district.

Fictional prototype. Journey times, providers and messages are simulated. No real booking, payment, hospital or clinical decision integration. Staff supply clinical requirements; the role selector is a demonstration control.
