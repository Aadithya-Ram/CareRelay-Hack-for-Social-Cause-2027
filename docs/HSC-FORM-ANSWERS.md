## 3. Theme Description & UN SDG Alignment
Characters: 466 / 500
CareRelay addresses healthcare access by coordinating disrupted dialysis appointments and accessible transport in Tamil Nadu. It supports SDG 3.8 (access to essential health services) and SDG 10.2 (inclusion regardless of age or disability), focusing on older adults, wheelchair users and families travelling from underserved areas. A proposed Coimbatore pilot would measure confirmation times and completed care journeys; clinical impact has not yet been validated.

## 4. Problem Statement
Characters: 476 / 500
When a dialysis appointment is cancelled or transport falls through, Tamil Nadu patients and caregivers may have to call centres separately, check staffed machine slots, explain accessibility needs and arrange an affordable journey. A nearby hospital listing does not confirm suitable availability. Fragmented treatment and travel coordination leaves families uncertain about where to go, when to arrive and who has accepted, especially when travelling from underserved areas.

## 5. Solution Description
Characters: 480 / 500
CareRelay checks staffed dialysis slots and suitable transport together, ranking eligible options by preferred-time fit and distance. Coordinators approve; hospitals and drivers confirm. Patients track progress, message authorized partners and retain history. Built with React/TypeScript, Workers, D1 and R2, the open-source prototype targets faster coordination. A proposed institution-hosted pilot would measure confirmation time and completed arrangements before wider rollout.
