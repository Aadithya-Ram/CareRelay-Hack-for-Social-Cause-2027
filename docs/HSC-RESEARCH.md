## Current official criteria matter more than guesses

The supplied *HSC-27-Competition-Guidelines.pdf*, pages 12–13, lists relevance, technical strength, functionality, creativity/innovation potential, social-cause impact, and presentation/team synergy. Pages 8–10 specify source/README/architecture/sample data/license and the 6–7-slide deck. Pages 13–15 require AI and prior-publication/submission disclosure. See the [current official HSC page](https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/hack-for-social-cause); it contains some older FAQ deadlines alongside a later main timeline, so confirm the active portal before submitting.

## How this submission responds

- **Local problem:** proposed focus on disrupted dialysis care and accessible transport around Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. This is a pilot hypothesis, not a claim of completed local field research.
- **Working interaction:** patient request → ranked manual/automatic choice → admin approval → hospital/driver confirmation → patient progress → closure/history.
- **Specific differentiation:** coordinate treatment and suitable travel with shared eligibility/reservation checks and explicit commitments; do not claim to have invented scheduling or replaced existing programmes.
- **Expected impact:** measure confirmation time, compatible arrangement rates, cancellations/unresolved reasons and coordination burden in a supervised pilot. Software tests do not prove clinical benefit.
- **Credible implementation:** scoped role workspaces, dated machines and vehicles, safe conflict handling, inboxes and reproducible tests. Scale limits and unfinished deployment foundations stay disclosed.
- **Clear future scope:** Tamil/assisted-access workflows, recurring schedules and verified integrations before extension to other scheduled care.

## Public-service and SDG context

The [Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme](https://pmndp.mohfw.gov.in/en) already addresses dialysis access and affordability. CareRelay proposes a complementary coordination workflow; it is not affiliated with or integrated into PMNDP and does not assert that an equivalent official capability is absent everywhere.

The proposal aligns with [SDG 3.8](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal3) on access to essential health services and [SDG 10.2](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal10) on inclusion, including age/disability. These are alignment goals, not measured progress against official SDG indicators.
